# Business Insights Memo - Retail Sales Performance

Period covered: 2014–2017 
Prepared by: Dunith Athukorala 
Source: Retail Business Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## Summary

Total sales of 2.30M generated 286.41K in profit (12.5% margin) across roughly 5,000 orders.

## Key Findings
1. Regional concentration. West and East regions account for the majority of sales, while South consistently underperforms the other three regions. This is worth investigating further, it may reflect market size, or a gap in sales execution or coverage in that region.
2. Balanced category mix. Technology leads at 36.4% of total sales, followed closely by Furniture (32.3%) and Office Supplies (31.3%). No single category dominates or is at risk, the portfolio is fairly diversified.
3. Discounting is eroding margin. Profit drops sharply as discount level increases. Bookcases is the clearest example: at a 21% average discount, it is the only sub-category running a net loss (-3,472.56 total profit). Binders carries the highest average discount across all sub-categories (37%) and remains profitable, but represents the next-highest risk if discount levels rise further.

## Recommendations
- Cap Bookcases discounts below 15% and review its pricing strategy directly, the current discount level is actively destroying margin on this sub-category.
- Monitor Binders discount levels. Currently profitable, but it carries the highest discount exposure in the portfolio and should be watched closely.
- Investigate the South region's underperformance relative to West and East, determine whether this is a demand-side issue (smaller market) or a sales execution issue (coverage, staffing, pricing).
